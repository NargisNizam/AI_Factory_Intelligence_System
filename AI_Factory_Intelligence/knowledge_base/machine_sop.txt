FACTORY MACHINE SOP — PRESS-01
Section 2.1: Normal operation
Temperature should normally remain below 78 C. Vibration should remain below 4.5 mm/s.
RPM should be operated within the validated production range.

Section 3.2: High-temperature response
If temperature exceeds 85 C, reduce machine load and notify the supervisor.
If temperature exceeds 92 C, stop the machine and inspect cooling and lubrication.

Section 4.1: Vibration response
Repeated vibration above 6 mm/s requires inspection of bearings, alignment and mounting.
Do not restart after a critical vibration event without supervisor clearance.

Section 5.0: Maintenance
Record the incident, machine ID, observed symptoms, action taken and supervisor decision.
